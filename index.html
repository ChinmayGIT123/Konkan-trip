Day-by-day plan

Driving times are planning estimates, not live traffic predictions. Open each route in Google Maps before leaving.

DAY 1

Pune → Guhagar

Approx. distance: ~230–250 km
Driving: about 6–7 hours, plus breaks
Suggested route: Pune → Bhor/Mahad → Chiplun → Guhagar (check live routing)
6:30 AM — Start from PuneCheck traffic and road conditions before departure.
En route — Breakfast & breaksPlan comfort stops; allow extra time for ghat roads.
Early afternoon — Arrive in GuhagarCheck in, lunch and take a rest.
Evening — Guhagar BeachRelaxed beach walk and sunset if weather permits.
Route: Pune → Guhagar
DAY 2

Ganpatipule + Pawas

Plan: Guhagar → Ganpatipule → Pawas → Guhagar
Driving: allow roughly 4–5+ hours total, depending on chosen roads and stops
Morning — Drive to GanpatipuleStart after breakfast; allow a generous travel buffer.
Visit — Ganpatipule Temple & beachTemple visit and a short coastal stop.
Lunch — Ganpatipule / en routeKeep this flexible based on opening times and comfort.
Afternoon — PawasVisit Shri Swami Swarupanand Ashram; check visiting hours.
Evening — Return to GuhagarIf the day feels too long, skip optional stops rather than rush.
Route: Guhagar → Ganpatipule → Pawas → Guhagar
DAY 3

Guhagar → Pune

Approx. distance: ~230–250 km
Driving: about 6–7 hours, plus breaks
Target: Pune by 5 PM; timing is not guaranteed
6:30 AM — Leave GuhagarEarlier departure gives more buffer for traffic and stops.
En route — Breakfast & breaksFollow live navigation and avoid rushing.
Afternoon — Arrive in PuneDo not add sightseeing on the return day.
Route: Guhagar → Pune
Route reality check: Ganpatipule and Pawas on the same day can make Day 2 long. Google Maps may suggest different roads and times depending on traffic, ferry/road conditions and closures. Review the route on the day; if it looks too tiring, choose Ganpatipule or Pawas rather than forcing both.
